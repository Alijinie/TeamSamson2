    QUESTIONS

    Before you get in touch

    On the day

    Officers arrive early, walk the venue once more, take positions, and the team lead checks in with you or your representative. You should not have to chase anyone.

    If plans change

    Timings slip and guests run late. Call the team lead — one number, not many. We reshape positions, routes and holding arrangements around the new plan.

    Afterwards

    We review what worked, note incidents or near-misses, and update the plan. For ongoing cover this becomes a scheduled review with reports.

    HOW A DETAIL IS ORGANISED

    One lead, clear roles

    • Team lead — your single contact; briefs the officers and stays reachable throughout.
    • Close officers — stay with the person; watch outward, not at the guest.
    • Advance & route — check venues, entrances, exits and parking before arrival.
    • Protocol hosts — receive, guide and seat guests; keep the programme moving.
    CODE OF CONDUCT

    What you can expect of us

    • Dress to fit the occasion — no unnecessary attention or display.
    • Respect privacy: no photographs of you or your guests, no discussion of your affairs.
    • Work with, not against, your venue, church, family or company security.
    • Keep positions and attention on the space around you, from arrival to handover.
